Attendance Records

Every personnel registration is automatically transferred here as an attendance record: date, time in, break out / break in, time out, and hours worked. GMP violators (Not Permitted) are not included, since they must register again before entering. These records are kept even if Monitoring Records are cleared.

Weekly Grid shows Sunday to Saturday: ✔ = came in (with hours worked), — = no record. GMP violators (Not Permitted) are not counted here; they are listed in GMP Violators and must register again before entering. Hours Worked = Time In to Time Out minus break time (“in progress” until timed out). Each Company / Agency starts on a new page when printed.

Daily Report

Shows all personnel registered on the selected date, with declared loose items, violations, and verifier signatures. In the print dialog, choose “Save as PDF”.

Monthly Trend

Compares the selected month with the previous month and shows the last 6 months. Re-registered violators are counted once, same as the other reports.
